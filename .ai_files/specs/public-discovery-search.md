# Spec: Public Discovery / Search

## Problem Statement
Visitors browse approved sites by category/tag, search, and open detail pages.

## User Stories
1. As a visitor, I want the index of approved sites with filters and pagination.
2. As a visitor, I want search suggestions (sites + tags).
3. As a visitor, I want related sites on detail (category today; tags preferred later).

## In Scope
- `index`, `search_suggestions`, `website_detail`, display limits (tags 10, featured 6, …)

## Out of Scope
- SEO meta/sitemap (Phase 2)
- View counts

## Open Questions
- Featured policy beyond random
